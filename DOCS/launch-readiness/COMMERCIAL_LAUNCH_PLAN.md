# Commercial launch plan

## Gate sequence

1. Technical private preview: close security/RLS/dependency gates and prove critical journeys.
2. Invite-only beta: validate recommendation usefulness, weekly household value, support burden, AI cost, and billing in test mode before limited real transactions are approved.
3. Single-market launch: only after legal/tax/privacy/accessibility review for that market and production operations are exercised.
4. Additional markets/languages: require the market matrix, translation QA, regional payments/tax, and support readiness to turn green independently.

## Funnel taxonomy

Measure `landing_view → signup_complete → onboarding_complete → first_recommendation → recipe_saved → meal_planned → grocery_list_used → return_week → paid_conversion → cancellation`. De-duplicate by event ID, document versions, and exclude ingredient, allergy, health, free-text prompt, and payment data unless strictly required and consented.

## Economics evidence required

Track active/paying households, retention cohorts, conversion, churn, revenue per payer, AI cost per active/paying user, payment fees, support burden, and infrastructure cost. Pricing, acquisition efficiency, market size, and contribution margin remain hypotheses until actual approved data exists. No public launch, price activation, marketing spend, or traction claim is authorized by this package.
